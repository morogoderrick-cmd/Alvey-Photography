About Alvery

Based in Eldoret.
Booked across three continents.

Alvery Photography is dedicated to capturing life’s most meaningful moments through our lenses. We are an Eldoret-based photography and videography studio covering weddings, graduations, anniversaries, choir productions, livestreams and everything in between. We travel for bookings across Kenya, the UK and Mainland Europe, and tailor every package to the occasion and the budget.

Affordable, dependable, and built around getting the shot that matters — without getting in the way of the day.

Good To Know

QUICK ANSWERS

Which days are you available?

We take bookings six days a week. We are unavailable on Saturdays.

Do you travel for events?

Yes. We are based in Eldoret and take bookings across Kenya, the UK and Mainland Europe. Terms and conditions apply for international bookings.

How much does a session cost?

Every booking is quoted to the occasion, hours and deliverables. Call, WhatsApp or send the form and we will give you a package price.

How do I receive my photos and videos?

Edited photos and video are delivered to a private online gallery you can view and download from.

Can you livestream or record my event?

Yes — livestreaming services and solo and choir video production are part of what we offer.
